Your progress

Streaks, times, saved games and coins are kept on your iPhone. If you're signed in to iCloud, they're also saved to your iCloud account, so they come back after a reinstall and follow you to a new iPhone. Without iCloud they stay on this iPhone only, and deleting the app deletes them.
